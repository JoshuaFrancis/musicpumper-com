What’s inside

A real working document, not a playlist.

Every issue arrives as a 64 page PDF and a 90 minute audio mix. Open it on a flight, read the notes on a train, queue the tracks in your booth. Built to be used, not scrolled.

    Fifty tracks, fully credited.

FAQ

Reasonable questions, plain answers.

Is it actually free? +

Yes. Four issues a year, free, forever. No upsell, no trial, no premium tier. The list itself is the product.

How often will you email me? +

Once a quarter, the day the issue drops. That is it. No drip sequence, no abandoned cart emails, no offer to upgrade.

Who curates it? +

A rotating panel of forty working DJs and label heads. Every contributor plays at least one paying gig a month. The masthead changes each issue.

Will my email be sold or shared? +

No. We never sell, share, or rent the list. Unsubscribe is one click, in every email, no questions asked.

Can I get past issues? +

Yes. The moment you sign up, you get a private archive link with all thirteen back issues and their mixes.
